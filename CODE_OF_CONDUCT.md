# Community Code of Conduct

SundayDeck exists to serve volunteers and congregations with humility, patience, and generosity. We ask everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, and translations to uphold a welcoming, gracious standard:

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with non-technical church volunteers sharing feedback or asking setup questions.
- **Assume good intent** and critique ideas constructively, never people.
- **Respect every congregation's worship tradition** — Protestant, Catholic, Mainline, Evangelical, Bilingual, and small church plants alike.
- **Respect copyright and attribution** — never upload copyrighted worship songs, hymnbooks, or Bible translations without verifiable open-license permission.

Harassment, personal attacks, spam, or bad-faith disruption will not be tolerated. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to keep this space safe and welcoming for everyone.
