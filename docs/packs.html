Switch Without Starting Over

Bring your church's existing song library in seconds.

Open Settings → Bibles & Packs → Migrate Library (or click Migrate Existing Songs on your first launch) to import directly from:

EasyWorship & OpenLP

Select EasyWorship's Songs.db (keep SongWords.db in the same folder) or OpenLP's songs.sqlite. SundayDeck cleans formatting automatically and keeps every stanza intact.

ProPresenter, FreeShow & VideoPsalm

Select exported .pro6, .show, .vpc, or OpenLyrics .xml files to import multi-slide songs straight into your offline library.

SongSelect, ChordPro & Bibles

Import SongSelect .usr/.txt, ChordPro sheets (chords stay off the sanctuary screen), numbered hymn sheets, and full 66- or 73-book offline Bibles (USFM, OSIS, USFX, Zefania, SQLite).
