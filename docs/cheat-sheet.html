Print this single page and tape it next to your booth laptop — everything a first-time volunteer needs.

Running Live Slides

    Click any item on the left to preview it safely without disturbing the sanctuary screen.
  • Next slide (or reveal next bullet)Space
  • Previous slideLeft Arrow
  • Show staged preview on screenCmd/Ctrl + Enter
  • Fix a lyric typo live (no flash)Cmd/Ctrl + E

Calm Recovery Keys (Press Again to Resume)

  • Hide Words (keeps background)F1
  • Black Screen (prayer or video)F2
  • Show Church Logo (welcome/end)F3
  • Instant hard cut (no fade)Shift + F1 / F2 / F3

Fast Lookup During Service

  • Bible Shorthand: Type jn 3:16, ps 23:1-3, sal 23, joao 3:16, or wis 3:1 and press Enter.
  • Hymnbook Jump: Type BH 330, UMH 378, HC 15, or #42 in the Songs search box.
  • Spoken Verses: When Auto-Follow is on, spoken Bible references wait safely in the right-hand queue — click once to stage.

5-Minute Pre-Service Checklist

  • 1. Displays: Click Projector in the top bar → open Audience Screen (and Stage Screen if used).
  • 2. Logo Check: Press F3 to confirm your church logo appears cleanly on the sanctuary screen.
  • 3. Review Order: Click through the Service Order on the left to verify songs, Scriptures, and announcements.
  • 4. Peace of Mind: You cannot break anything — Preview never changes what the room sees until you click Show on Screen.

Using Microphone Auto-Follow Safely

Click Auto-Follow in the top bar → pick your room mic or soundboard feed → start in Suggest next slide (highlights the upcoming slide with an amber ring without moving automatically). Auto-Follow automatically pauses whenever Black Screen (F2) or Show Logo (F3) is active.
